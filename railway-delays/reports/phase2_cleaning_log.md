# Phase 2 cleaning log: Corridor B

Generated by `python -m src.data.clean` (functions in `src/data/clean.py`). Raw files in `data/raw/` are never modified.

## Assumptions

- **Every train runs daily.** The raw file has no day-of-week information (weekly/bi-weekly trains are treated as daily).
- `day` is the 1-based journey day of the row's times. A halt crossing midnight has one `day`, so its departure is rolled to the next day.
- Row order within a train is the raw `id` order (consecutive within each train).
- A row with only one time (away from origin/terminus) gets the other time (zero halt). Rows with neither time are dropped. Nothing else is invented.
- Anchors: NDLS, TDL, ETW, CNB, ALD, MGS (Old Delhi dropped). A corridor train has a schedule row at 2+ anchors. **The raw schedule lists pass-through stations with arrival = departure**, so this is 'passes or stops', not 'halts at'. Counting only real halts (halt > 0) gives 217 of the 229 trains.

## Core stations

- 122 stations are served by 20+ corridor trains; 12 of them are off-line and were removed (see `data/processed/corridor_stations_offline.csv`), leaving **110** core stations.
- The earlier note of 24 off-line stations was my miscount: only 15 were ever listed. Three (DLI, DSA, VVB) fell out of the 20+ set by themselves once DLI stopped being an anchor, so 12 were removed explicitly.
- Stations per segment between anchors (inclusive of both anchors): NDLS - TDL: 33; TDL - ETW: 11; ETW - CNB: 19; CNB - ALD: 30; ALD - MGS: 21.
- Main-line chain (NDLS to MGS): 110 stations, branch/loop stops not on the chain: none.
- 17 trains visit chain stations out of chain order. All are around Prayagraj (ALD, NYN, COI): the stop sequences (e.g. NYN ALD NYN, or NYN ALD COI) suggest trains reversing at or looping around Prayagraj Jn, so ALD-NYN-COI looks like a triangle rather than a straight chain (inferred from the sequences, not checked on a map). Trains: 11045, 11046, 12149, 12175, 12176, 12177, 12178, 12295, 12322, 12791, 12792, 16359, 16360, 18609, 18610, 19305, 19306.

## Train types

Rules (first match wins):

```
1. name contains RAJDHANI -> Rajdhani
2. name contains SHATABDI -> Shatabdi
3. name contains DURONTO -> Duronto
4. number starts with 5 (passenger series), or name contains PASSENGER/MEMU/DEMU -> Passenger
5. number starts with 12 or 22 (superfast series), or name contains ' SF'/SUPERFAST -> Superfast (this includes Sampark Kranti and Garib Rath, which carry superfast numbers)
6. number starts with 1 or 2, or name contains EXP/MAIL -> Mail/Express
7. anything else (e.g. 0xxxx specials) -> other
```

Types come from the name and number only, not from `trains.json`. Coach-slip entries (name or number contains 'Slip') are flagged `is_slip`; they duplicate the movement of another train.

## What may trouble the simulator

- **Pass-through rows.** Only 1380 of 15873 core stop rows are real halts (`is_halt`); the rest are pass times. Halts are whole minutes, mostly 0.
- **Whole-minute times vs short segments.** 218 consecutive-stop segments take 0 minutes and 256 take 1 minute. Straight-line speed on the other 15426 has median 74 km/h; 485 exceed 130 km/h (max 525, from 1-minute segments of a few km) and 196 are under 10 km/h (e.g. KNS-PJY, 0.12 km apart, 16-22 min; cause not investigated). Do not use per-segment speed from this data; use whole-section times or add track distances.
- **Trains that skip the main line.** 39 trains have two consecutive core stops more than 60 km apart (39 hops; longest 870 min, e.g. 14003/14004 Delhi-Farakka jump Ghaziabad to Mughalsarai, 677 km). They touch two anchors but run between them on a different line (for example via Lucknow-Varanasi, or Prayagraj to Mughalsarai by another route). Flag `leaves_main_line` in `trains`; a simulator that moves them along the main line chain would be wrong for these.
- **Revisits.** 6 trains visit a core station twice (Prayagraj reversal); sequence keeps both visits.
- **Mid-route entry/exit.** 163 of 229 trains enter the corridor away from their origin and 163 leave before their terminus, so their incoming delay is unknown.
- **No per-stop distance** in the raw file; `segment_diagnostics` uses straight-line distance from station coordinates.
- **Daily assumption** inflates traffic: weekly/bi-weekly trains run every day in the simulation.
- **Old timetable** (pre-2018 names/codes, no recent trains). Freight is absent.
