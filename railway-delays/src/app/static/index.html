🔗 Delay Inheritance

Delays along Corridor B are predominantly inherited rather than newly generated in open sections. Strict headway constraints mean a delayed lead train directly impedes follower services.

⚠️ Critical Chokepoints

Propagation cascades heavily concentrate around Etawah–Ekdil (ETW-EKL) and Kanpur–Chandari (CNB-CNBI). Priority at these nodes yields network-wide relief.

🌫️ Weather & Congestion Regimes

Under dense winter fog or high traffic congestion, downstream cascade propagation increases significantly, multiplying delay persistence across 1 to 6 hours ahead.
